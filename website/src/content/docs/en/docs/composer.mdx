<span id="composer" />

The composer sits at the bottom of a session. On the left are image, model, and approval pills; on the right, Send or Stop. While a turn runs, sending queues a message. The status bar below summarizes tokens, cost, approval mode, branch, and model.

<span id="composer-send" />

## Send, queue, and stop

Write in the composer and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to send; use <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> for a new line. Send is disabled when the draft and image list are empty. The first send starts the turn and adds your message plus the agent’s replies, thinking, and tool calls to the timeline.

While the turn runs, Send becomes Queue and the message waits above the composer. Click Stop or press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to end the turn. Each queued message can be sent now or removed; <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> steers the most recent queued message into the current turn.

The status bar accumulates input/output tokens and cost; when a branch is known it appears there, with the cwd on hover.

<span id="composer-model" />

## Switch models and thinking levels

Click the model pill at the bottom-left of the composer (“provider · model · thinking”) to open a searchable model list. Type a provider or model name to filter, then click an entry to switch; the bottom of the popover holds thinking levels for the current model. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>M</kbd> opens the same picker, as does the model name at the right of the status bar.

Models cannot be switched while a turn runs; those entries are disabled until it settles.

<span id="composer-approval" />

## Change the approval mode

Next to the model pill is the approval pill. Open it to choose Supervised / Auto-accept edits / Full, or press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> to cycle. A role stores the default for new sessions; changing it in a session applies from the next tool call that needs approval.

Operations waiting for you appear in the approval bar above the composer with Deny / Allow for session / Allow. Pending approvals from other workspaces also raise a banner near the top of the window; “Review and resolve” opens them together. See [Approvals](/en/docs/approval/).

<span id="composer-images" />

## Images, history, and drafts

Use the image button at the left of the composer or paste with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd>. PNG, JPEG, GIF, and WebP are supported; up to 10 images per message, 20 MB each, 50 MB total. Thumbnails appear above the composer and can be removed with the ×; an image-only message is allowed.

With an empty composer or the caret on the first visual line, <kbd>↑</kbd> recalls the previous sent message; on the last line, <kbd>↓</kbd> moves forward. <kbd>Esc</kbd> returns to your draft. Editing recalled text turns it into a new draft. Drafts are kept per session, so switching away and back preserves them.
